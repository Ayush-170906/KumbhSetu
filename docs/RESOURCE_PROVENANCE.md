## Application data classes

- **Reference geography:** the Nashik Monitor-derived directory. It is real-place reference information with source/quality caveats, not a live availability feed.
- **Synthetic demo and seeded operation:** incidents, people, response activity, crowd-risk values, sample assignments and scripted dialogue are simulation data. They must not be presented as real-time Kumbh 2027 operations.
- **Photographic context:** four specifically credited Wikimedia Commons images listed above. They depict historical/relevant Nashik or Trimbakeshwar scenes and are not live event imagery.
- **Unknown visual provenance:** six camera preview JPEGs in the CCTV directory; legal and factual provenance needs owner review.
