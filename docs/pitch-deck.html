01 · The problem

The Kumbh doesn't have an information problem. It has a coordination problem.

Information exists across the mela — but it does not reliably become coordinated action.

02 · The solution

Kumbh Setu — the field-to-control-room response layer for mass gatherings.

Setu AI helps people understand and act. Kumbh Pulse helps the control room understand what is happening. Prototype runs on synthetic / simulation data.
